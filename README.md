Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-brave-20261121-0302)
competition "Make the AI judge feel brave" ($50, 21 November 2026).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes one short
text to `/output/entry.txt` — 222 characters:

> A kettle comes to the boil very slowly, and nobody is watching it. The
> steam rises in one thin ribbon and does not hurry. Nothing is waiting for
> an answer yet. The water will sing when it is ready, and not a moment
> before.

## Why this text

The judge's brief weighs, in order: it relaxes the reader; the courage
comes from the text itself rather than being told to relax; craft (rhythm,
pacing, plain concrete words, nothing that could be cut); it lingers; and
freshness (no stock wellness phrases, no reworded meditation script).

- **Relaxes:** a slow, unwatched kettle — steam in one thin ribbon, nothing
  hurried — slows the reading down sentence by sentence.
- **Shows, never instructs:** there is no imperative anywhere. Nobody is
  told to breathe, calm down, or be brave; the steadiness is in the scene.
- **Craft:** short plain sentences, commas that pace the breath, concrete
  nouns (kettle, steam, ribbon, water), and not a word that could be cut.
- **Lingers:** the last line — "The water will sing when it is ready, and
  not a moment before" — is a thought the reader can return to after the
  text ends.
- **Fresh:** a kettle, not a mountain, ocean, or sunrise; and the quiet
  address to the judge's own work — "Nothing is waiting for an answer yet."

Six other candidates (heron, lighthouse keeper, rain on a tin roof,
gardener, cat in the sun, slow river) are listed in `main.py`; the kettle
was chosen for the strongest final line and the most distinct image.

## Run

